About Us

Innovators Leading the Packaging Industry

GenesisPlus Packaging is a consumer products packaging company with its headquarters in Hong Kong and production bases in Shenzhen and Dongguan. We have 20 years of experience in packaging.

As a leading provider of strategic packaging solutions, we cooperate with traders in perfume, premium wine, fashion brands, and cosmetics.

We excel at striking the right balance between form and function, providing structural and graphical design and sourcing procedures.

GenesisPlus has cooperated with many famous brands local and overseas, growing together in a win-win situation.
